About Us

The Acton Citizens' Band, formed in 1872, is comprised of local musicians of all ages who volunteer their time to bring the joy of music to the Acton community and surrounding area.

As both a concert band and a marching band, we make many appearances each year including several feature concerts, Canada Day celebrations, the Acton Fall Fair, and the Santa Claus Parade.

Practices are held every Wednesday from 7:30 pm to 9:30 pm at the Ted Tyler Music Centre on Wallace Street.

For inquiries about joining the Acton Citizens' Band, or to request a performance at your event, please reach out to us at actonband@gmail.com. We would love to hear from you!

Our Bandmaster

Carin Pelka, a cherished member of the Acton Citizens' Band for many years, stepped into the role of conductor and bandmaster in 2022, bringing a wealth of musical expertise and dedication to the position. Under Carin's leadership, we continue to grow and thrive as a band, delivering elevated performances for our community. We are grateful to have Carin as our Bandmaster, guiding us with her boundless enthusiasm, talent, and commitment to creating beautiful music together.

Learn With Us

Music has the power to connect people, enrich lives, and unite communities. The Acton Citizens' Band celebrates this universal language which nourishes personal growth, inspires creative expression, and fosters lasting bonds between members. By starting a new journey with us, not only will you reap the therapeutic and cognitive benefits of learning music, but you will also be contributing to a historic community tradition.

Our Junior Band initiative provides free music lessons, books, and instrument loans, giving you everything you need to start or enhance your musical journey. We will guide you until you are ready to join the rhythm of the Citizen's Band.

Whether you are exploring the world of music for the first time or you are an experienced musician keen to learn a new instrument, you are welcome in the Junior Band.

Join Us

You are invited to join over 150 years of tradition and community spirit with the Acton Citizens' Band! Our band has a rich history of bringing music to Acton and the surrounding area, and we would be honoured to have you as part of our legacy.

We offer both a marching band experience and a concert band experience, providing our members with a diverse range of performance opportunities. From marches to musicals, we embrace a wide variety of genres, ensuring that there is something for everyone to enjoy. You will have the chance to showcase your talents in a variety of settings, creating memorable moments for both yourself and our community.

In the spirit of inclusivity and the promotion of learning music, we loan instruments free of charge to all our members. Whether you are a brass player, woodwind enthusiast, percussionist, or have a passion for conducting, there is a place for you in the Acton Citizens' Band. Come and experience the joy of making music with us while proudly representing our beloved community of Acton.
